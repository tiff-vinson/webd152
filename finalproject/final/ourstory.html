Our Founder

Here at Pacific Trails Resort, we owe our founding and legacy to the woman who started it all: Tiff Vinson.

In 2025, she had become fed up with the constant targeting of Californian beauty like Big Sur and the surrounding areas. Not only that, but the average cramped office job couldn't quite contain the size of Tiff's true dream: a cool and cozy resort where everyone can enjoy what the Pacific Coast has to offer. With the support of her wife and friends, she managed to pull the resources together and create the beginnings of Pacific Trails Resort!

By 2026, Pacific Trails Resort had gotten the attention of Governor Gavin Newsom and his youth advisory committee. Looking to uplift and highlight the region's effervescent views and comforts, they gave Tiff a special grant that propelled the resort to even bigger heights, improving our speciality yurts and expanding our activities.
